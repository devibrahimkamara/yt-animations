🧩 About
AI Influencer 2.0 is Higgsfield's end-to-end workflow for creating a virtual influencer and producing social-ready content around that character — from first design to final video to multiple creative variations, all inside one platform.
Link to Higgsfield AI Influencer 2.0: https://higgsfield.ai/ai-influencer
💥 Key Capabilities
📌 Full Character Builder: Design your AI influencer from scratch using dedicated controls for gender, age, ethnicity, facial features, skin, eyes, hair, body type, accessories, and more — no restrictions, no predefined "normal." Human and non-human characters both supported.
📌 Add Your Face: Start from your own identity. Upload your image and build a character based on your own likeness — then take it anywhere.
📌 Saved Character Identity: Once built, your character is saved and reusable. No rebuilding from scratch every generation — the same persona travels across every piece of content you make.
📌 Genjutsu Motion Transfer: The video engine powering AI Influencer 2.0. Select a built-in trend or upload your own reference clip (3–30 seconds), and Higgsfield recreates that performance — body movement, timing, camera work, and scene action — starring your AI influencer.
📌 Element Swapping: After generating a performance, swap out clothing, products, backgrounds, or objects without changing the core motion. One shoot, many variations.
📌 Character Consistency Across Content: Higgsfield's Soul / Soul ID system keeps your character recognizable across different outfits, environments, lighting, and concepts — essential for building an ongoing virtual presence.
📌 Images + Video in One Workflow: Move from character creation → consistent images → social video → motion recreation → content variations, all inside Higgsfield.
